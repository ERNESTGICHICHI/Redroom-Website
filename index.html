Our Story

More Than a
Nightclub

The Red Room is Nairobi's most intimate and electric nightlife destination. Born from a passion for world-class entertainment and curated experiences, we've created a space where music, art, and culture collide.

Every night is carefully orchestrated — from hand-selected resident DJs spinning Afrobeats, Amapiano, and international sounds, to live performances by homegrown talent and international headliners.

Our cocktail program is built by expert mixologists who treat each drink as a statement. The ambiance is designed to feel exclusive yet electric, intimate yet alive.
